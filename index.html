INTRODUCTION

A founder at the intersection of creativity, technology, and business.

I'm Faruq Ayomide Ojolowo, known as Burti 🧃 — a Nigerian entrepreneur, creative, and founder based in Lagos.

My interests began with art and animation and gradually expanded into technology, entrepreneurship, AI, automation, business, and intellectual property.

Today, I'm building Apex Universal Studios™ while continuing my education and developing the creative, technical, business, and leadership capabilities required for the larger vision I'm working toward.

01 — WHAT I'M BUILDING

I don't want my work to be limited to one medium or one professional identity. I'm developing across animation, visual design, technology, AI, automation, software, programming, business, management, art direction, writing, worldbuilding, and decision-making.

03 — ABOUT FARUQ

The story so far.

Faruq Ayomide Ojolowo was born on 28 June 2003 in Iragbiji, Osun State, Nigeria.

His early interests were rooted in art and animation, with art and music becoming important influences on his creative identity.

As he grew older, those interests expanded toward technology and entrepreneurship.

One early influence was his older brother's work in web development and software engineering, including his experience owning a technology company. Seeing technology being turned into something practical and commercial gave Faruq an early understanding of what could be built with it.

University

In 2023, Faruq began his university education at the Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA).

He initially studied Physics, but eventually recognized that it did not align with the direction he wanted to pursue.

He changed his academic direction to Business Information Technology (BIT), returning to 200L and adding an additional year to his university journey.

His academic path reflects a broader interest in the intersection of technology, business, and creative work.

His expected graduation year is 2028.

Finding the intersection

During his early university years, exposure to a UI/UX course strengthened his interest in the relationship between technology, design, and creative production.

From there, much of his development became self-directed.

Animation eventually became the creative direction through which many of these interests began to converge.

That eventually led to Apex.
