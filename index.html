I build companies around ideas that don’t fit in one medium.

A single world can be a film, a comic, an album, a game and a piece of software. Each one is its own craft, and each is done best by people who specialise in it. I build companies so those crafts can grow properly on their own, while the thread connecting them stays intact.

Apex Universal Studios™

I’m the Founder and CEO. It’s the company I’m building now.

Apex Universal Studios™ is the parent company I founded. It brings entertainment, media, technology, intellectual property and creative businesses under one identity.

It’s organised as one parent with specialised divisions inside it. The parent holds ownership, identity, standards and long-term direction. The divisions build their own creative, technical and business systems.

The divisions

Apex Animation Studio™
The animation division. Animated films, series and shorts, along with character animation, visual development and production.
Apex Pictures™
The visual storytelling and publishing division. Comics, graphic novels and illustrated storytelling, plus the original IP that grows out of them.
Apex Music™
Music creation and releases, including albums, singles, recordings and soundtracks, and music-driven visual projects.
Apex Labels™
The artist and label side of music. Artist development, releases, catalogue management and the business operations behind them.
Apex Games™
Video games and interactive experiences, including the technology and systems that support them.
Apex Software™
Software, digital products, creative technology, internal tools and automation. It supports the other divisions and can build independent products too.

How they work together

The structure exists so work can move between divisions.

  • An animated property can become a comic.
  • A game can use music made inside the company.
  • An artist developed through the label can contribute to a larger project.
  • A character that starts in one medium can appear in others.

Crossing divisions doesn’t merge their identities, and not every idea needs to cross.

What it stands for

Apex Universal Studios™ works from ten principles: originality, creative excellence, ambition, meaningful entertainment, long-term thinking, ownership, innovation, adaptability, collaboration and respect for the work.

It’s just as clear about what it doesn’t chase. It doesn’t need every project to be bigger than the last, every new technology adopted, every IP expanded, or every market entered. Growth isn’t worth giving up creative identity for.

Where it’s heading

The aim is an enduring, globally recognised creative enterprise built on strong original IP, creative technology and a range of distinct businesses. That is a direction, not a forecast.

The structure is designed so new divisions can be added without rebuilding it.

What comes next
