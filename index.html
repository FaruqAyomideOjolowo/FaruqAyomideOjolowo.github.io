A little about me

The person behind the work.

I’m Faruq Ayomide Ojolowo, also known as Societyloveburti. I’m an entrepreneur, founder and creative with interests that stretch across business, technology and creative industries.

My journey

My interests started with art and animation, then grew into film, storytelling and design. Over time, technology and entrepreneurship became part of that picture too. Those interests continue to influence what I explore, what I learn and the things I choose to build.

Today, I’m building Apex Universal Studios™, my first company, while continuing to develop my understanding of business, creative work and technology. My identity is bigger than one company or one professional title, and I want this space to reflect that.

I enjoy looking at ideas from different angles. I value originality, independent thinking, ownership, curiosity and the discipline to keep improving. I’m not interested in pretending to have everything figured out. I would rather keep learning, make thoughtful decisions and do the work.

Outside business and technology, I’m drawn to music, films, comics, graphic novels, games, architecture, cars, travel and basketball. Different interests often give me new ways to think about creativity, people and the world around me.

Looking ahead, I want to build meaningful work and ventures over the long term, keep expanding what I can do and turn worthwhile ideas into something real.

Entrepreneurship and ongoing work

What I’m building.

My entrepreneurial interests extend across creativity, technology and business. Apex Universal Studios™ is my first company and my current venture.

Current venture

Apex Universal Studios™

A diversified entertainment, media and technology company I founded. Its focus is on developing original intellectual property, creative work, stories, characters, fictional worlds, digital products and experiences.

First company · Current focus ↗
One company, distinct areas of work.

Apex is being developed through specialised divisions, each with its own creative or technological focus.

The divisions are at different stages of development. They are not all operating at full scale yet.

Beyond one venture

Apex is my current focus, not the limit of my entrepreneurial interests. I expect to explore other businesses and industries as worthwhile ideas develop. My aim is to keep learning, build with intention and create businesses, intellectual property and capabilities that can grow over time.
