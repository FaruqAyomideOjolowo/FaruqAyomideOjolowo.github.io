About Me

The Beginning

I grew up in Iragbiji, Osun State, Nigeria. My childhood was shaped by music, art, and the constant question: how do you build something that lasts? My older brother—a software engineer and entrepreneur—showed me the blueprint. He didn't just code. He built companies. He thought long-term. He understood that technology is a tool for creating systems, not just solving problems.

That perspective changed everything.

The Path

I started with animation and digital art. Self-taught. I was obsessed—spending nights studying motion, color theory, composition. Then I expanded into graphic design, music production, then into the deeper layers: software engineering, AI, automation, art direction, worldbuilding. These aren't separate disciplines. They're all threads in the same tapestry: building systems that amplify human creativity.

I studied Physics at Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA), but that wasn't where the real learning happened. The real education came from building. In 2023, I made a choice: I founded Apex Universal Studios™. It's the culmination of everything I'd learned about creativity, technology, and systems.

The Cycle: Create → Develop → Operate → Own → Expand → Preserve → Build Again

This isn't linear. It's cyclical. You create something. You develop it into a product or service. You operate it, refine it, learn from it. You own the systems and the IP. You expand into adjacent spaces. You preserve what works. Then you build again—bigger, smarter, more resilient. That's Apex.

How I Approach This

Everything I do is guided by a set of core principles. They're not nice-to-have values—they're non-negotiable. They shape how I make decisions, who I work with, and what I build.

Originality

The world doesn't need another remix. It needs genuinely new ideas. Everything I build has to be original thinking—new ideas, new approaches, new worlds. If it's been done before, I'm not interested.

Independence

Freedom to think and act without external compromise is foundational. I don't build for trends or for what others think I should build. I build for what I believe in.

Ownership

Full responsibility. No excuses. If something I built fails, it's on me. If something succeeds, I own it completely. That clarity matters.

Excellence

Every detail counts. The small things compound. I polish until it shines. Mediocrity is the enemy of impact.

Innovation

Constantly asking: what if? How do we do this better? What hasn't been done yet? Innovation isn't a feature—it's a mindset.

Long-Term Thinking

Short wins are noise. I build for decades, not quarters. The best systems take time to compound.

Ambition

Big goals. Relentless execution. No settling for less than what's possible.

Discipline

Ambition without discipline is just noise. Discipline is what turns ambition into results. Consistency compounds.

Freedom

The ability to choose what I work on—and who I work with—is sacred. Freedom enables the best work.
