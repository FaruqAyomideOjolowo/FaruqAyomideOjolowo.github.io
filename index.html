About Me

A founder at the intersection of creativity, technology, and business.

I’m Faruq Ayomide Ojolowo, known as Societyloveburti — a Nigerian entrepreneur, creative, and founder based in Lagos.

My interests began with art and animation and gradually expanded into technology, entrepreneurship, AI, automation, business, and intellectual property.

Today, I’m building Apex Universal Studios™ while continuing my education and developing the creative, technical, business, and leadership capabilities required for the larger vision I’m working toward.

02

What I'm Building

Beyond a single discipline.

I’m interested in what happens when creative work, technology, business, and intellectual property are treated as parts of the same system.

That idea sits at the center of how I think about building.

I don't want my work to be limited to one medium or one professional identity. I’m developing across animation, visual design, technology, AI, automation, software, programming, business, management, art direction, writing, worldbuilding, and decision-making.

The objective isn't simply to collect skills.

It is to become capable of understanding enough of the different parts of a larger system to make informed decisions, work with specialists, recognize opportunities, and build something that can grow.

Apex Universal Studios™

The company I'm building.

Apex Universal Studios™ is the company I founded and lead.

What began from an interest in animation developed into a broader company vision combining:

  • Entertainment
  • Media
  • Technology
  • Artificial intelligence
  • Automation
  • Business
  • Intellectual property
  • Creative development

Apex is not intended to remain a traditional animation studio.

The long-term direction is to build an interconnected creative and technology ecosystem capable of creating original intellectual property, developing businesses around that IP, using technology intelligently, and expanding into new mediums and opportunities.

Apex Vision Includes

Original IP

Creating original characters, stories, worlds, franchises, and creative properties.

Entertainment

Developing animation, film, comics, games, music, and other entertainment experiences.

Technology

Using AI, automation, software, programming, and technical systems to expand creative and operational capabilities.

Business

Building companies, products, systems, and opportunities around the intellectual property and capabilities developed within the ecosystem.

The larger idea is that creative work, IP, technology, and business reinforce one another.
